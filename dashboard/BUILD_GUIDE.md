# Build the dashboard yourself in under 15 minutes

`dashboard.html` is the finished reference: open it first so you know what you are aiming for.
A `.pbix` file can only be created by Power BI Desktop itself (it cannot be generated by script), so these steps recreate the same
**4 KPIs + 4 visuals** from the ready-to-import files in the `data` folder next to this guide.

**Which tool?** You are on a Mac: Power BI Desktop is Windows-only, so use **Option A (Tableau Public, free, runs on Mac)**.
Use **Option B** if you have a Windows PC.

## The files (all in `dashboard/data/`, one-click import, no cleaning needed)
| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `kpis.csv` | the 4 KPI cards (text already formatted) |
| `risk_summary.csv` | Visual 1: churn-risk customer count |
| `dept_repeat.csv` | Visual 2: reorder rate by department |
| `volume_by_week_since_first_order.csv` | Visual 3: order volume over time |
| `top10_products.csv` | Visual 4: top repeat-driving products |
| `customer_risk.csv` | optional: one row per customer, for slicers |

**Titles to type for each visual** (they state the finding, so the dashboard reads in 30 seconds):
1. Churn risk: 3 in 10 customers have just had a month-long gap
2. Fresh staples are the habit: dairy and produce are re-bought ~2 of 3 times
3. Orders taper steadily after a customer's first weeks
4. Bananas, strawberries, avocados and spinach drive repeat orders

**Colours (optional):** healthy / habit = `#1B7F79`, high risk = `#E07B39`, watch = `#E8B84A`.

## Option A: Tableau Public (Mac or Windows)

**Setup**
1. Download **Tableau Public** (free) from public.tableau.com/app/discover > "Create" > "Download the app". Install and open it.
2. On the start screen under **Connect**, click **Text file** and pick `kpis.csv`. A data preview appears.

**Sheet 1: KPIs**
3. Click **Sheet 1** (bottom-left). Drag `kpi_label` onto **Columns**. Drag `kpi_display` onto **Text** in the Marks card (it sits under Dimensions). You now see 4 tiles. Right-click the sheet tab > Rename: `KPIs`.
4. To make the numbers big: click **Text** in the Marks card, then the **...** button, select the text and set the size to 20 or more. Optional: right-click the grey column header area and untick **Show Header** if you want only the numbers.

**Sheet 2: Churn risk**
5. **Data > New Data Source > Text file** > `risk_summary.csv`. Click a new sheet tab (icon at the bottom). Drag `risk_tier` to **Rows**, `customers` to **Columns**. Drag `risk_tier` to **Color**.
6. To order High / Watch / Healthy: right-click `risk_tier` pill > **Sort...** > Sort by **Field**, field `tier_sort`, aggregation **Minimum**, ascending.
7. Drag `customers` onto **Label** (Marks card) so the counts show. Rename the sheet `Risk`.

**Sheet 3: Reorder rate by department**
8. **Data > New Data Source > Text file** > `dept_repeat.csv`. New sheet. Drag `department` to **Rows**, `reorder_rate_pct` to **Columns** and also to **Label**.
9. Click the **sort descending** icon (bars with an arrow) in the toolbar. Rename the sheet `Departments`.

**Sheet 4: Order volume over time**
10. **Data > New Data Source > Text file** > `volume_by_week_since_first_order.csv`. New sheet. Drag `week_since_first_order` to **Columns**, then right-click that pill and choose **Continuous**. (If the option is missing, right-click the field in the left list > **Convert to Dimension**, then drag it again and choose **Continuous**.) Drag `orders` to **Rows**.
11. In the Marks card dropdown change **Automatic** to **Area**. Rename the sheet `Volume`.

**Sheet 5: Top products**
12. **Data > New Data Source > Text file** > `top10_products.csv`. New sheet. Drag `product_name` to **Rows**, `times_reordered` to **Columns**, `reorder_rate_pct` to **Label**. Click the sort descending icon. Rename the sheet `Products`.

**Assemble and save**
13. Click the **New Dashboard** icon (bottom, grid with a plus). Set **Size** (left panel, bottom) to **Automatic**.
14. Drag `KPIs` to the top. Drag `Risk`, `Departments`, `Volume`, `Products` below in a 2x2 grid.
15. For each sheet double-click its title on the dashboard and type the matching title from the list above.
16. **File > Save to Tableau Public As...** and sign in (free account). Your link opens the finished dashboard.

## Option B: Power BI Desktop (Windows only)

**Setup**
1. Install **Power BI Desktop** (free) from the Microsoft Store. Open it, click **Blank report**.
2. **Home > Get data > Text/CSV**. Pick `kpis.csv` > **Load**. Repeat for `risk_summary.csv`, `dept_repeat.csv`, `volume_by_week_since_first_order.csv` and `top10_products.csv` (5 loads). Stay in **Report view** (first icon on the left).

**KPIs**
3. In **Visualizations**, click the **Multi-row card** icon. Tick `kpi_label` and `kpi_display` in the **Fields** list (right). Set `kpi_display` to **First** (click the arrow beside it in the field well > First). Stretch the card across the top.

**Visual 1: Churn risk**
4. Click empty canvas. Click **Clustered bar chart**. Drag `risk_tier` to **Y-axis**, `customers` to **X-axis**.
5. Sort order: open **Data view** (second icon on the left), click the `risk_summary` table, click the `risk_tier` column, ribbon **Column tools > Sort by column > tier_sort**. Back in Report view the bars read High, Watch, Healthy.
6. Turn on **Format > Visual > Data labels**.

**Visual 2: Departments**
7. Click empty canvas > **Clustered bar chart**. `department` to **Y-axis**, `reorder_rate_pct` to **X-axis** (it shows as Sum, which is fine because there is one row per department). Click the **...** at the visual's top-right > **Sort axis > reorder_rate_pct** and **Sort descending**. Turn on data labels.

**Visual 3: Order volume**
8. Click empty canvas > **Area chart**. `week_since_first_order` to **X-axis**, `orders` to **Y-axis**. In **Format > X-axis > Type** choose **Continuous**.

**Visual 4: Top products**
9. Click empty canvas > **Clustered bar chart**. `product_name` to **Y-axis**, `times_reordered` to **X-axis**, `reorder_rate_pct` to **Tooltips**. **...** > **Sort axis > times_reordered** > **Sort descending**.

**Finish**
10. For each visual: select it > **Format > General > Title** > on, and type its title from the list above.
11. Arrange the KPI card on top and the four visuals in a 2x2 grid. **File > Save as** `retention_dashboard.pbix`.
